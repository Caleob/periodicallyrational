📜 What is Scansion?

Scansion is the art of marking the stresses in a poetic line to reveal its rhythmic heartbeat. Shakespeare primarily wrote in Iambic Pentameter (5 feet of unstressed / stressed pairs, u /).

However, he often varied the rhythm with Trochees (/ u), Pyrrhics (u u), or Feminine Endings (extra unstressed syllables) to highlight emotion or character shift.
