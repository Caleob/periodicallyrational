By Caleob King • Software Post-Mortem & ELA Scansion Case Study • October 2026

Can a STEM educator use AI to build a great English Language Arts tool? To find out, I built The Bard's Meter—an interactive Shakespearean prosody sandbox. In this retrospective essay, I examine what went right mechanically, the subtle pedagogical dangers of treating poetic scansion like binary math, and why deep subject expertise remains irreplaceable even in an era of effortless code generation.
