Why Visual Proofs Matter in Middle & High School

Rigorous formal proofs often intimidate students before they have developed spatial and conceptual intuition. By starting with dynamic, interactive demonstrations—where parameters can be dragged, stretched, and tested—students formulate conjectures organically before writing a single line of symbolic logic.
