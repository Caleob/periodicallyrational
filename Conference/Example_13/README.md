# Example 13: The Bard's Meter (Shakespeare Scansion Interactive)

## What is this app?
This is a theatrical, Elizabethan-styled interactive web app designed to teach students the rhythm and meter of Shakespearean verse. Students watch velvet proscenium curtains part to reveal poetry lines, guide a bouncing dot syllable-by-syllable using keyboard hotkeys to assign stress, analyze meter classifications (such as iambic pentameter or trochaic variations), reflect on why poetic variations exist, and export a detailed, timestamped student performance report.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Theatrical Whole-Class Hook:** Launch the app on a shared screen. The rising SVG stage curtain, vintage typography, and illuminated proscenium arch immediately hook student curiosity and set a dramatic mood.
* **Kinesthetic Syllable Stresses:** Use the keyboard arrow keys (Up for stressed [/], Down for unstressed [u], Left/Right to navigate) or numeric hotkeys (1 and 0). The bouncing target dot gives an active, game-like rhythm feel to scanning verse.
* **Scaffolded Three-Stage Rhythm Analysis:**
  1. *Stage 1 (Scansion):* Mark unstressed/stressed syllables across the verse.
  2. *Stage 2 (Meter Classification):* Identify the prevailing foot and meter count (e.g., Iambic Pentameter, Trochaic Tetrameter).
  3. *Stage 3 (Literary Reflection):* Prompt students to analyze *why* Shakespeare broke strict meter in specific lines to mirror a character's emotional turmoil or psychological unraveling.
* **Extensible via Custom JSON:** Teachers can upload their own custom JSON poem datasets, adapting the workshop for sonnets, Romantic poetry, modern hip-hop lyrics, or AP Literature review sets.
* **Accountability & Assessment:** Students receive a rhyming Elizabethan farewell screen and download a timestamped `.txt` report documenting time on task, accuracy percentage, and their written literary reflections.
