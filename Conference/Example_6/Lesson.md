# Lesson 6: Scaffolding Stages & External APIs

### 1. Gate Later Steps Behind Earlier Ones
* In complex problems, early mistakes compound into total confusion.
* Tell the AI to lock later steps until the foundation is verified: Stage 1 (Name the angle) must pass before unlocking Stage 2 (Identify relationship) or Stage 3 (Solve the algebra).
* Catching misconceptions immediately keeps students from practicing errors.

### 2. Advanced Move: Borrowing Other People's Engines (APIs)
* You don't have to build every complex feature from scratch.
* This tool connects to the **Graspable Math API**—an interactive algebra engine written by someone else.
* Unlike our earlier self-contained rules, this *does* reach across the internet to load an outside library.
* As your prompting skills grow, plugging into third-party tools lets you build world-class capabilities into your own custom interface.
