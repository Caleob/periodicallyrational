# Example 1: EduSpin Wheel Picker

## What is this app?
This is a fun, colorful digital spinning wheel that runs right inside your internet browser. You can use it as a single wheel to pick one thing at random, or put two wheels side-by-side to pair things up or compare ideas.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Random Student Caller & Partner Matcher:** Use a single wheel to call students at random, or use the dual-wheel Partner Matcher with fair cross-removal to pair students up without repeats.
* **Classroom Job Matcher:** Spin students against classroom duties (Line Leader, Tech Director, Timekeeper) to assign roles transparently and enthusiastically.
* **Inquiry & Perspective Analysis:** Pair essential questions with analytical historical lenses (Economic, Social Class, Cultural, Subaltern Voices) or compare vocabulary concepts side-by-side.
* **Fair Turns:** Turn on the setting to remove names after they are picked so nobody gets picked twice.
* **100% Student Privacy:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or upload student names to the cloud. Your class roster stays safely on your own computer.
