# Lesson 7: Orchestrating Media & Arcade "Game Feel"

### 1. Leveling Up to Real Media Files
* Earlier we avoided outside media; now we can manage real audio and image files.
* Keep your audio files (`.mp3`) and images (`.png`) in the same folder as your code files.
* Prompt the AI to reference the exact filenames: *"Play `Turret_Fire.mp3` when clicking, and loop `Playing_Music.mp3` during gameplay."*

### 2. Browser Autoplay & Audio Etiquette
* Browsers will strictly block audio from playing automatically until the user clicks something on the page.
* Always design a **"Start Game"** or "Clearance" screen so the user's first click unlocks the sound.
* Always include separate, visible mute toggles for Music and Sound Effects for classroom volume control.

### 3. Add "Juice" (Game Feel)
* Don't settle for boring static math drills—ask for sensory arcade feedback.
* Prompt for specific physical reactions: *"Add a 200ms screen shake on impact"*, *"turret recoil kickback"*, and *"exploding particle effects"*.
* These visceral cues transform repetitive coordinate plotting into an exciting challenge.
