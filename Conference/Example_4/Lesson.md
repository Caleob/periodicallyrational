# Lesson 4: Designing for Screen Sharing & Asymmetric Roles

### 1. Build for Your Meeting Platform
* Design your tools around the exact features of your video platform (Class/Zoom, Teams, Meet).
* **Outgoing:** One-click copy buttons formatted for instant private chat with student leaders.
* **Incoming:** Feed platform exports (poll results, chat logs, attendance CSVs) directly into your tool to instantly connect with live attendees.

### 2. Design for Secrets (Teacher View vs. Student View)
* Many classroom games require hidden information or an answer key.
* Ask for a "Teacher Peek" toggle with subtle visual cues so you can manage the game without spoiling answers on screen share.

### 3. Use Unicode & Emojis for Instant UI Graphics
* You don't need custom image icons or heavy graphics packs.
* Prompt for simple emojis and symbols (🔴, 🔵, 💀, ⚙️) to give buttons and game cards instant, colorful clarity.
