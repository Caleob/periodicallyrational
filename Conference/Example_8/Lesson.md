# Lesson 8: Frameworks vs. Vanilla & Heavy Math

### 1. Frameworks vs. Vanilla (Two Ways to Build)
* AI often defaults to modern frameworks like **Tailwind CSS** or **React** because they make rapid styling easy.
* **The Catch:** Frameworks often rely on outside libraries or build tools that can break or get blocked by school firewalls.
* **The Vanilla Way:** Native HTML/CSS/JS needs zero installs, no compilers, and runs offline forever. If you want maximum reliability, always tell the AI: *"Use pure Vanilla code with zero external dependencies."*

### 2. The Browser as a Scientific Calculator
* You don't need expensive graphing calculators or paid math software to do college-level math.
* The browser can calculate advanced formulas on the fly (p-values, degrees of freedom, normal and Chi-square distributions).

### 3. Connect Numbers Directly to Pictures
* Don't make students look up abstract numbers in textbook tables.
* Prompt the AI to draw the dynamic curve and shade the rejection region in real time.
* Seeing the test statistic line land visually inside or outside the shaded zone makes the math instantly click.
