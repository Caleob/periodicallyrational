# Example 2: FeedbackFlow (Grading Feedback Builder)

## What is this app?
This is a point-and-click grading assistant. Instead of typing the same comments over and over on 80 student papers, you click buttons for the grade levels and pre-set comments you want. The app instantly turns your clicks into a warm, complete paragraph of feedback that you can copy and paste with one touch.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Speed Up Grading Without Sounding Like a Robot:** Keep this open on half of your screen while viewing student work on the other half. Click through criteria like "Main Idea", "Evidence", or "Grammar", and watch a smooth paragraph form right before your eyes.
* **Quick Common Notes:** Add quick one-click buttons for phrases you use all the time, such as "Please check office hours for help" or "Remember to cite two sources."
* **Grade Summary Included:** The updated version can automatically calculate scores (like 10/12) and attach a tidy breakdown at the end of the note.
* **Save Your Rubrics:** You can save your rubric to a small backup file on your computer and open it again whenever that assignment comes around next term.
