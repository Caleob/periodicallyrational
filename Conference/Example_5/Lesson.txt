# Lesson 5: Breaking Down Complexity (PRDs & The Web Triad)

### 1. The Web Triad: Skeleton, Clothes, and Brain
* As tools grow more complex, cramming thousands of lines into one file gets messy and confuses the AI.
* Split your project into three specialized files:
  * **HTML (`Elements_On_Screen.html`):** The skeleton—buttons, textboxes, and canvas shapes.
  * **CSS (`How_It_Looks.css`):** The clothes—colors, dark mode themes, fonts, and card spacing.
  * **JavaScript (`What_It_Does.js`):** The brain—math calculations, interactive cords, and animations.

### 2. Don't Let AI Guess: Write a PRD
* A one-sentence prompt like *"Make me a geometry game"* forces the AI to guess the math, the rules, and the layout—usually badly.
* Write a **Product Requirements Document (PRD)** first: outline the pedagogical rule (Altitude-to-the-Hypotenuse), the exact controls (red/blue/yellow cords), and the step-by-step animation.
* You can ask the AI to help you draft the PRD before writing a single line of code!

### 3. Animate the "Aha!" Moment
* Textbooks show static, confusing diagrams that students struggle to mentally rotate.
* Prompt for multi-stage animations (slide, flip, rotate) that physically demonstrate proof right before their eyes.
