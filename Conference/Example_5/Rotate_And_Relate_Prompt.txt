Act as an expert educational front-end engineer and geometric visualization specialist. Build a zero-dependency, interactive web manipulative called "Rotate & Relate: Similar Right Triangles" designed to help secondary geometry students master the Altitude-to-the-Hypotenuse Theorem through tactile side-matching and kinematic transformation animations.

### Technical Stack & Architectural Triad:
Split the implementation into 3 clean, modular standalone files with zero external dependencies (no React, D3, or Tailwind; pure HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript):
1. `Elements_On_Screen.html`: Semantic HTML5 markup, SVG container scaffolding, UI cards, and modal elements.
2. `How_It_Looks.css`: Modern "Dark Cyber-Geometry" theme (#0a0a0f canvas, #12121a cards, glowing neon accents: cyan #38bdf8, gold #fbbf24, violet #a855f7), responsive layout, and typography.
3. `What_It_Does.js`: Procedural geometry generator, SVG coordinate rendering, pointer-driven patch cord physics, similarity verification engine, and multi-stage transformation animator.

### Pedagogical Core: Altitude-to-the-Hypotenuse:
When an altitude is drawn from the right angle of a right triangle to its hypotenuse, it creates two smaller sub-triangles that are similar to each other and to the parent triangle.
The tool must randomly generate a primary right triangle (vertices labeled A, B, C with altitude to point D), select two of the three triangles (Large, Medium, or Small), and challenge students to:
1. Match corresponding sides using 3 color-coded virtual patch leads.
2. Formulate the exact geometric similarity statement in correct corresponding vertex order.
3. Trigger an animated transformation proof that translates, reflects, and rotates the triangles into concurrency to verify their work visually.

### Interactive Components & Behavior:

1. Dynamic SVG Geometry Workspace:
   - Procedurally generate a non-isosceles right triangle with altitude $CD \perp AB$.
   - Display angle markers ($90^\circ$ squares) and distinct vertex labels.
   - Render the two selected triangles to be compared.

2. Color-Coded Patch Cords (Drag & Connect):
   - Provide 3 virtual patch cords:
     * Red Cord: Connects corresponding Short Legs.
     * Blue Cord: Connects corresponding Long Legs.
     * Yellow Cord: Connects corresponding Hypotenuses.
   - Use unified Pointer Events (`pointerdown`, `pointermove`, `pointerup`) with live SVG quadratic bezier curve rendering while dragging between side midpoint anchor pins.

3. Similarity Statement Builder:
   - Provide interactive dropdowns or text inputs for the similarity statement:
     $\Delta \text{ [ _ _ _ ] } \sim \Delta \text{ [ _ _ _ ] }$
   - Ensure the student must match vertices in exact corresponding order (e.g., matching right angle to right angle, acute to acute).

4. The Kinematic Transformation Animation (The "Aha!" Moment):
   - When the student clicks "Check & Animate":
     * Stage 1 (Translation): Glide the second triangle across the canvas until hypotenuse midpoints coincide.
     * Stage 2 (Reflection): If the two triangles have opposite spatial chirality, smoothly reflect across the axis.
     * Stage 3 (Rotation): Rotate the second triangle into concurrency so it nests directly inside or coincides with the first.
   - As the triangles align, if the patch cords were connected correctly, the cords shrink into glowing alignment rings. If incorrect, mismatched cords visibly twist and flash amber/red.

5. Controls & Feedback:
   - "New Problem": Generates a new randomized right triangle configuration.
   - "Reset Cords": Clears connected leads.
   - Real-time score and streak tracking.
