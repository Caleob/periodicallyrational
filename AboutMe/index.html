The Mission of Periodically Rational

Math and science knowledge is a triumph of humanity. These hard-won secrets of the universe are our common legacy. But as our knowledge grows, the project of transmitting these ideas and passing that torch to the next minds so that they can carry it even further becomes increasingly challenging. Luckily, the tools of technology give us new and creative ways to demonstrate the concepts that produced them! This website is built in the hopes we can share tools, techniques, and strategies for STEM education focusing on:

  • Accessible classroom software: Lightweight, zero-install apps that run smoothly in any browser without requiring third-party logins or data collection.
  • Visual mathematical intuition: Giving students dynamic ways to manipulate angles, equations, and functions.
  • Teacher empowerment: Demonstrating how educators can design their own custom classroom tools quickly and without complex coding overhead.
